# Templates

clusterctl templates released with the provider: two `Cluster` flavors,
the `noop` `ClusterClass`, and the identities they reference.

See [Templates and ClusterClass](https://captf.io/docs/user-guide/clusterclass.html)
for the flavors, generating a cluster, the `ClusterClass` and its topology
variables, and template immutability; see
[clusterctl Variables](https://captf.io/docs/reference/clusterctl-variables.html)
for every variable and its default.
